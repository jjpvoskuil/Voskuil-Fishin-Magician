Privacy policy

Voskuil Outdoors — covers the Fishin Magician and Deercast apps. Last updated November 10, 2026.

Who runs these apps

Fishin Magician (bass fishing forecasts and trip logs for Nolin River Lake, Kentucky) and Deercast (hunting forecasts and hunt logs) are private, non-commercial apps run by John Voskuil for himself, family and invited friends. They are not open to the public: only people the owner has added can use them.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google we receive your Google account's email address, name and profile picture. We use your email only to check that you have been invited and to label the records you create. We do not receive your Google password and do not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

The apps store what you enter: fishing trips, catches, lures and tackle, fishing spots, hunting stands and hunt logs, notes, photos you upload, and location points you choose to record. Forecasts use public weather, moon and lake data; your location is used only while the app is open and only for the features you use.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Data is stored with Supabase (a hosted database) and is only readable by signed-in, invited users, according to each app's sharing rules (for example, anglers on Fishin Magician see each other's trips for the leaderboard). Photos are kept in private storage. Backups are kept in a private GitHub repository.

What we don't do

We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone, show ads, or use it for anything other than running these apps.

Removing your data

Email jjpvoskuil@gmail.com to have your account and the records you created deleted. You can also remove the apps' access from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
